# Standard sheep-ovary R-first workflow

Use this profile when the query is sheep/Ovis/ovine ovary and a full-feature Seurat RDS is available. It fixes execution boundaries, not biological labels or selected resolutions.

## Preprocessing boundaries

A StereoPy-converted `cellbin_PPed` object is a raw-count carrier unless independently proven otherwise. Fresh verified same-batch whole tissue uses SCT v2/glmGamPoi, 4,000 HVGs and BANKSY `M=0`, `k_geom=30`, `lambda=0.2`, 30 PCs, Leiden k=50 with the full `0.2,0.4,0.6,0.8` grid. A reusable BANKSY input retains its exact bound grid.

Every second-round initial-cluster cohort restarts from the same project's non-SCT raw counts and uses SCT/PCA/SNN/Leiden with `0.1,0.2,0.3,0.4,0.6`. PCs and k are selected from current-cohort evidence. Never SCTransform SCT corrected counts, reuse BANKSY graph parameters or introduce a below-grid resolution to repair a defective graph.

## Ordered biological phases

1. **Whole-tissue cohort partition.** Select a stable grid member that preserves major follicular, vascular, surface and stromal anatomy without technical fragmentation. Record each initial cluster as provisional broad, mixed or unknown, plus watch signals. Create exactly one second-round cohort per initial cluster. Do not write biological or QC labels.
2. **Independent second-round reclustering.** For every cohort compute the complete Leiden grid, DEG, pseudobulk, spatial morphology and full-catalog evidence. Select by stable identity-program separation and anatomy; compare the nearest neighboring resolutions. Score while provisional and historical labels are hidden.

   Bind reproductive-stage evidence once before the second round. The controller must pass it to both whole-subcluster adjudication and local mixed-subcluster closure. It enables evaluation of `Luteal` candidates but cannot assign them. The same biological group must contain direct multigene joint steroidogenic/corpus-luteum identity support, a direct corpus-luteum discriminator and positive query-local DEG contrast; corpus-luteum-like morphology then validates the identity. Luteal cannot use the dominant whole-subcluster shortcut.
3. **Subcluster adjudication.** Return a coherent high-purity subcluster wholesale. Use `parent_return`, `cross_lineage_return` or `missing_broad_reconstruction` after score freeze. Record reliable fine/state proposals and stable catalog-external programs. Underpowered cohorts remain not evaluable rather than inheriting the provisional parent.
4. **Local mixtures.** Only a second-round subcluster with two material pairwise-exclusive direct identity components, or a material specific direct component inside a supported generic remainder, enters local splitting. Detection of several candidates, shared steroidogenesis/ECM/contractile signal or a stable 0.5%-level trace is a watch, not a mixed call. A clear specific parent is challenged only by an independently supported subcluster-level program with a candidate-declared direct discriminator. At least one neighboring higher-resolution Leiden partition must reproduce the proposed identities as enriched and separable components; then candidate-local expression/spatial observation components define the exact selected-subcluster boundary. A scattered tail not reproduced at higher resolution cannot trigger local splitting. Before any local job, audit the combined workload across every cohort and bind its PASS artifact to each local call; more than 50% pending stops dispatch and returns to resolution/trigger review. Propose candidates independently, resolve overlap by discriminators, extract specific lineages before generic Stromal remainder and rescore the exact local remainder once. Nonselection is not QC.
5. **Broad freeze.** Merge every cohort into an exact disjoint analysis-set cover. Freeze formal broad membership only here. Parent-lock fine proposals and keep hypoxia, proliferation, atresia, luteinization and other states in separate fields.
6. **Atlas and preliminary completeness.** Map all cells once after broad freeze with the immutable contract-bound GSE233801 split-wall v2 bundle. Enforce its class capability matrix: Granulosa, Immune, Stromal/mesenchymal, Endothelial, Pericyte/mural and Smooth muscle have independent supported prototypes; Epithelial/mesothelial and Theca are challenge-only; Oocyte and Luteal are unsupported. Rescue only unlabeled, current-query-class-calibrated, moderate-or-higher, non-OOD, context-evaluable and profile-compatible observations. Challenge-only predictions can trigger review but cannot write labels. Existing labels are not silently overwritten. Audit all present/absent catalog lineages, large-label embedded programs, unmodeled programs and every broad-by-fine candidate, but an open preliminary audit must not prevent the downstream biological reviews that can resolve it.
7. **Three-endpoint biological review.** Run `validate_sheep_ovary_biological_quality.py` on the post-Atlas membership and all disjoint second-round observation scores. Audit every broad label's spatial plausibility, canonical Oocyte quality and stage-appropriate follicle ROI histology. Detect Granulosa components and review Theca/Vascular/Smooth/Stromal programs across radial shells and angular sectors. A coherent hidden program triggers one bounded raw-count ROI/source-subcluster review; it never writes a label from geometry. No follicle or no antral follicle is `not_evaluable`, not failure.
   For a targeted repair, pass the frozen pre-iteration ROI review as `--expected-roi-review`; disappearance of a previously confirmed antral cavity is a hard biological rejection, not a successful `not_evaluable` result.
   If only the reopened ROI has fresh observation scores, pass the complete-section annotation as `--coordinate-membership`; this input contributes x/y only, never labels or assignment evidence.
   The standard controller performs at most one automatic ROI iteration, and only when all remaining failures are typed follicle ROI failures. It restarts every ROI from project-local non-SCT raw counts, replaces those ROI scores in the complete-section score ledger, and reruns the full three-endpoint review. A cropped ROI-only PASS cannot authorize completion.
8. **逐大类全样本复核.** After all Atlas/ROI changes, review every context-evaluable annotated broad strictly one at a time. The controller exposes one active target, one packet and one decision; the Agent announces `现在开始对 <cell type> 进行专项复核。` before interpreting it. Each review jointly evaluates current-label precision, whole-query recall, raw-count/full-transcriptome molecular identity, whole-section spatial consistency and literature-boundary alternatives. Source subclusters and components are internal evidence/patch bounds, not separate biological decisions. A group watch is raised when selected-resolution DEG/pseudobulk/multichannel evidence persists but observation-level direct seeds are too sparse; it must be localized with raw-count evidence and cannot write the whole subcluster. A question refuted by the bound multichannel packet can close without a patch; a confirmed defect requires exact cell IDs or a bounded source-cohort raw-count review. The audit cannot label cells. Retain, justified absence or exact patch closes that broad's only full-query specialist review. Later review of another broad may transfer exact evidence-bounded cells into or out of a closed broad, but records only a post-closure membership delta and never requeues the closed review. Reuse stable partitions and resume from the previous controller pause so Atlas/rescue/ROI/clustering are not repeated.
9. **Final materialization.** Proceed only after both the per-broad whole-query review and three-endpoint review are `PASS`. Convert the terminal unresolved remainder to typed QC. QC <10% and <50,000 remain formal-release requirements, but after one Atlas route, one bounded unresolved review, optional one ROI repair and exactly one full-query specialist decision per evaluable broad, a larger remainder stops as `PENDING_USER_REVIEW_HIGH_UNRESOLVED`; it cannot trigger another whole-residual or QC-anchor reclustering cycle. Build the lightweight review report from either the release-ready or explicitly pending membership; final release assets require user confirmation and a PASS release.

## Sheep-ovary biological boundaries

- The candidate catalog is open and no fixed broad list is required. Stage-, breed- and section-specific luteal, neural/glial or other programs may appear.
- Endothelial, Pericyte/mural and Smooth muscle are independent broad identities; Lymphatic endothelial is a high-confidence child of Endothelial, and `Vascular-associated` is legacy-only.
- Smooth muscle requires a mature nonvascular MYH11/CNN1/ACTG2-centered identity after mural exclusion; ACTA2/TAGLN or ring location alone is insufficient.
- Epithelial/mesothelial requires coherent epithelial identity, anti-program clearance and plausible surface/rete morphology; one keratin or one component cannot expand a mixed cluster.
- Oocyte uses the complete canonical-cluster route in `sheep_ovary_oocyte_rfirst_route.md`; zona/ambient signal and spatial adjacency cannot expand it.
- Theca requires an independent steroidogenic/androgenic program. Luteal requires compatible stage and corpus-luteum structure.
- Granulosa fine identities require independent stable programs; hypoxia, proliferation, atresia and luteinization are states by default.
- Mesenchymal progenitor-like and Neuroendocrine-like remain exploratory. Stable catalog-external programs remain unmodeled until reviewed.

## Output

The pre-confirmation report shows the final broad/fine census, full-section annotation, fixed-point high-contrast class highlights, absolute and within-gene-normalized marker dotplots from one source table, representative DEG, label-independent all-cell marker spatial maps, evidence summaries and unresolved review regions. Observation counts are not inferred histological cell counts.
