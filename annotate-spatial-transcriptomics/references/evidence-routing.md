# Evidence and unresolved-observation routing

## Evidence floor

Use coherent marker families, direct/local full-feature expression, DEG/anti-DEG, pseudobulk, neighboring-resolution stability, spatial morphology and technical/ambient alternatives. Status flags, paths, one marker and empty tables are not evidence.

The whole-tissue pass may compute these signals only to describe initial clusters and watches. It cannot convert them into cell labels. The authoritative score freeze occurs independently inside each second-round initial-cluster cohort with the provisional record hidden.

## Second-round outcomes

Every selected second-round subcluster is reviewed against the full open catalog and receives one structural outcome:

- whole-subcluster broad return (`parent`, `cross_lineage` or `missing_broad`);
- local mixed-subcluster trigger;
- fine/state/unmodeled proposal attached to a broad outcome;
- `unresolved_biological` or `underpowered_not_evaluable`.

High-purity subclusters return wholesale so sparse noncontradictory members inherit broad identity. A mixed subcluster may yield multiple disjoint lineage subsets plus a parent/unresolved exact remainder. Candidate order and aggregate winner never allocate overlap.

## Local mixtures

Use local observation scoring only after a trigger documents at least two separable competing identity cores in one exact second-round subcluster. Candidate components are independent; generic ECM, shared parent markers and ACTA2/TAGLN cannot bridge specific identities. Group thresholds validate an existing component rather than selecting cells one by one. Unselected cells are not QC.

## Merge and Atlas

All cohort and local outcomes merge into one exact disjoint analysis-set cover before formal broad labels freeze. Atlas is then run once over all cells at broad level.

For an unlabeled observation, calibrated moderate-or-higher, non-OOD and ontology/profile-compatible Atlas support can directly rescue broad identity. A defined broad is comparison-only. Material conflict or coherent OOD reopens the complete source subcluster/cohort once for full-feature query evidence; Atlas alone never overwrites it and never writes fine labels.

## Uncertainty

Retain `unresolved_biological` when competing programs cannot be separated, a cohort is underpowered, or a stable program is not yet represented in the catalog. Retain `Unmodeled lineage candidate` for a repeatable, spatially coherent, nontechnical program without a catalog match. Neither is early technical QC.

Only final materialization assigns typed `qc_holdout`, `qc_reject`, `unknown_candidate` or `technical_state`. A large final unresolved fraction returns to its contributing second-round cohort or post-merge review, never to first-pass whole-object per-cellbin threshold tuning.

## Contamination-prone signals

Require lineage-core programs and compatible anti-markers. Spatial adjacency, zona RNA, generic contractility or a single surface marker cannot override a coherent resident program. Report spots/cellbins separately from inferred biological objects.
